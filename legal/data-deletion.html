Data Deletion Instructions

EXE FB Monitoring (also known internally as "EZComment") — Last updated: September 2026

Any team member, or any Facebook user whose public comment has been retrieved by this App, may request that their data be deleted from our system.

สรุปภาษาไทย: พนักงานในทีม หรือผู้ใช้ Facebook ที่คอมเมนต์ถูกเก็บไว้ในระบบนี้ สามารถขอให้ลบข้อมูลของตัวเองออกได้

What data we store

  • Public Facebook post and comment content from our own Pages (message text, commenter display name where available, timestamps, attached media URLs).
  • Team member account data: email address, optional display name, and access role.
  • Internal activity logs of actions taken by our own staff (e.g. replies sent, comments archived).

How to request deletion

  1. Send a deletion request to the team administrator managing this App, including:
    • The Facebook comment link (permalink) or comment ID, if requesting deletion of a specific comment's stored copy; or
    • Your email address, if requesting deletion of your own team member account and associated activity records.
  2. The administrator will locate and permanently delete the specified record(s) from our database within a reasonable time.
  3. You will receive confirmation once the deletion is complete.

Automatic removal

If a comment is deleted directly on Facebook, our system does not automatically detect and remove the corresponding stored copy — a manual deletion request as described above is required to remove it from our records.

Contact

To submit a data deletion request, please contact the team administrator managing this App.
